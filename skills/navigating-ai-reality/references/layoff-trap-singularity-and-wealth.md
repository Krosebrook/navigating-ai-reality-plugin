# The AI Layoff Trap, Economic Life Expectancy, $44.4T Singularity Model & Vulnerable User Bias

- **The AI Layoff Trap (Tsoukalas & Falk)**: In a competitive market, rational CEOs face a Prisoner's Dilemma where automating and firing workers is a dominating strategy, eroding aggregate consumer demand unless external policy taxes full replacement while keeping human augmentation tax-free ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O3gYyCB2n9o), [Jacob Coxon NYC Council Testimony](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wRiFeGS30Aw)).
- **2-Year Remote Economic Life Expectancy**: Purely remote digital task execution faces a ~2-year life expectancy as agents operate at 1/100th cost and $1.50/hr humanoid robots threaten 1M US truckers + 3M service workers ([Emad Mostaque](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOIJ8m2rSC4)).
- **Anthropic Workforce & $44.4T IMF Singularity Report**: Inside Anthropic, junior technical hiring fell from 27% to 9% while agent-to-human hours hit 6:1 company-wide and 19:1 in Research ([Anthropic Workforce Report](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X8Qwp_naMzg)). In Anthropic's IMF Extreme Singularity scenario ($44.4T US GDP by 2030), knowledge workers (62.4% of jobs) see wages drop 11.5% (or >42% if flexible; 24% unemployment if sticky) while physical trades rise +33.6% and capital share flips from 40% to 55% ([Jack Clark IMF Lecture](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wS3lJpOs9Uw), [Bill Gates 20-Year Transition](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A0kTUP7fWtk), [Andrew Yang K-Shaped Economy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=020ZvO0FbMM)).
- **Targeted LLM Underperformance (AAAI 2026)**: Frontier LLMs hallucinate more and provide lower accuracy for users with lower English proficiency, lower formal education, or non-US origin ([Poole-Dayan, Roy, & Kabbara, arXiv:2406.17737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17737)).
