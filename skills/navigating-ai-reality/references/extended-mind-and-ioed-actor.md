# 7-Pillar Extended Mind Architecture, IOED+ & ACTOR Protocol

## 1. The Extended Mind Thesis & 7-Pillar Cognitive Prosthetic
Grounded in Andy Clark and David Chalmers' Extended Mind Thesis (1998) and Kyle Rosebrook's Workplace Systems Analysis ([The Extended Mind Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1YMRZeI8BUN-pXQo3LAJxvUWFqgk_ccmqUWoprtOMFKQ); [The Reliability Gap](https://drive.google.com/file/d/17sjh5svdtf6xe5sPA4FGweSDLBvr_iye)):
- **Core Axiom:** Cognitive Capability != Cognitive Reliability. A worker can have 99th-percentile diagnostic speed and 12th-percentile biological recall under interruption.
- **The 7 Pillars:**
  1. **Capture:** Log timestamps and raw events as they happen (eliminate retrospective memory).
  2. **Externalize:** Replace blank pages with structured templates (`[Root Issue | Action Taken | Next Step]`).
  3. **Remind:** Fire triggers at workflow boundaries (after a call ends), not passive lists.
  4. **Restore Context:** Maintain a **Session Resume Card** so context restores in 10 seconds after interruption.
  5. **Verify:** Enforce hard closure gates before marking any task complete.
  6. **Automate:** Offload clerical formatting while preserving human diagnostic authority.
  7. **Escalate:** Define clear rules for handing off to a human specialist.

## 2. Puncturing the Illusion of Explanatory Depth (IOED+)
- **Empirical Evidence:** Rozenblit & Keil (2002), [Chromik et al. (2021)](https://www.medien.ifi.lmu.de/pubdb/publications/pub/chromik2021iui/chromik2021iui.pdf), and [eScholarship ChatGPT IOED Research](https://escholarship.org/content/qt2m38s5xm/qt2m38s5xm_noSplash_5f8e687430eb3ee1aa9d724fb815943c.pdf) prove that non-technical users systematically overestimate their understanding of complex systems when given tidy local explanations or AI summaries.
- **Magnitude:** When required to write a step-by-step causal explanation, **48% (moderated) to 67% (unmoderated)** of non-technical participants drop their self-rated understanding.
- **The "Second Why" Forcing Function ([Case File KR-002](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1BOq522tdKMhAacoI1ZMew-vecQLgoXvc/view?usp=drivesdk)):** Never stop at borrowed jargon. Ask: *What is the first principle underneath? Where does this explanation break? What would falsify it?*

## 3. The 5-Step ACTOR Self-Education Framework
From [How To Become Dangerously Self-Educated (with AI)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VeU6gScy92s):
1. **A — Aim:** Write one sentence before reading: *"I am studying X because I need to decide/build Y."*
2. **C — Compress:** Isolate the **Trunk** (the single load-bearing causal mechanism) before collecting **Leaves** (quotes, examples).
3. **T — Test:** Use AI as an adversarial sparring partner to attack your hidden assumptions.
4. **O — Own:** Look away from the screen and explain the mechanism aloud in your own words.
5. **R — Run:** Convert the concept into one immediate rule, decision, or 7-day experiment.
